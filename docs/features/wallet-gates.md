# Wallet copy gates

**Status:** approved 2026-10-15.

**Problem.** The Wallet report judges a wallet from its last few hundred trades only, so a wallet that was great all-time and is losing this week, a launcher trading its own coins, or a thin four-token record can still read as copyable. The Discord feed Worker gains two allowlisted GMGN reads, **wallet stats** (7 and 30 days: realized profit, cost, win rate, token count, outcome buckets, GMGN's tags, followers, Twitter handle, coins launched) and **all-time profits**. The Wallet report gets a **Copy gates** block above Claude's verdict: four gates, each pass / fail / not measured with the one number that decided it, and a one-line verdict naming its cause, plus a record line ("120 tokens · 58% won · realized $8k in 7 days, $150k all-time · 4 over 5×"), who this is (GMGN's tags, Twitter handle, followers) and a size cap (half the wallet's own average buy). **Real record** fails on a launcher (more coins launched than half its tokens traded) or when one coin made 75%+ of its recent gains; fewer than 5 tokens, or a wash-trading tag this app cannot check, leave it unmeasured. **Still working** fails when the week is −10% or worse after +10% or better all-time, when both 7 and 30 days lost money, or when the all-time record never made money. **Reachable** fails on an MEV or sandwich tag, a median entry market cap under $30k, 10k+ followers trading sub-$1M caps, over 100 trades a day, an average buy under $50, or a median hold under a minute. **Cuts losses** fails when 35%+ of its tokens are down more than 50%. The verdict follows GMGN's order: No read (no trades in 7 days, or too few tokens), Do not copy (record or week fails), Hold off (something unmeasured), Watch, do not copy (unreachable), Copy the buys, not the exits (does not cut), Copyable at small size (all four pass). The gates also go into Claude's prompt so its verdict agrees with them. No new dependency, no key in the front end, no Claude cost for the rules. We know it works from unit tests on stats and profits shaped like GMGN's documented answer (each gate's pass, fail and not-measured case, the verdict order, a launcher, a cooled star, a thin sample, the one-coin test), Worker tests for the two new reads (the POST body and query, the parameter checks, holdings never forwarded) and a browser test with faked GMGN showing the block, the verdict, the fold line, the prompt lines and the unmeasured case.

Approach and thresholds credit: GMGN's wallet-analysis agent skill (github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills). Their script also reads the wallet's holdings for the one-coin test, hold-to-zero positions and wash-trade corroboration; that read needs a signing private key, which this app does not keep anywhere, so those parts are either done from the Wallet report's own trades (one coin) or left unmeasured (hold-to-zero, wash) and the block says so.

## Rules

- Logic in `walletgates.js` (pure, shared with the tests): `record` (GMGN's 7-day stats, 30-day stats and all-time profits to one record; the 7-day stats are a must, the rest optional), `concentration` (the biggest closed gain's share, needing 3+ winners among 8+ closed trades), `gates`, `verdict`, `sizeCap`, `recordLine`, `identityLine`, `promptLines`. UI in `walletreport-ui.js` (`wrReadGates`, `wrGatesHtml`).
- Reads, through the paced GMGN queue (Worker first, the phone's key as a fallback): `user/wallet_stats` with `period=7d` and `period=30d` (GET) and `user/wallet_profits` with `period=all` (a POST to GMGN: the Worker and the phone route turn the app's GET into it, with the wallet in the JSON body and only `timestamp` and `client_id` in the query). The Worker's allowlist (`worker/feed-core.js`) and the app's list (`gmgn-ui.js`) both name them; `user/wallet_holdings` stays out (it needs a signing key).
- Field names come from GMGN's published `gmgn-cli` (`realized_profit`, `bought_cost` / `total_cost`, `realized_profit_pnl` / `pnl`, `buy`, `sell`, `pnl_stat.token_num`, `pnl_stat.winrate`, `pnl_stat.pnl_gt_5x_num` … `pnl_stat.pnl_lt_nd5_num`, `common.tags`, `common.twitter_username`, `common.followers_count`, `common.created_token_count`; profits: `list[0].total_realized_profit`, `list[0].total_realized_profit_cost`). A stats answer may be an object or a one-row list. They are not yet confirmed on a live wallet from the build machine; the block says "GMGN wallet stats not read: …" with GMGN's reason if a read fails.
- A return is GMGN's own ratio when given, else profit ÷ cost; no cost and no ratio is unknown, never 0.
- Not measured never counts as a pass: the verdict says Hold off and how many gates were not measured. A failed gate speaks before an unmeasured one.
- Fold line: the rule verdict's label, then Claude's label and style when read.
