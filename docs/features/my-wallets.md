# My wallets

**Status:** approved 2026-10-16 (owner: "I'm multi walleting", 5 GMGN wallets; Paper trading and the Calls tab stay as they are).

**Problem.** Trading from several GMGN wallets meant the Home tiles, Stats and Sizing only ever showed one of them, and keeping each synced meant switching to each and tapping Sync. (1) Each linked wallet gets a **Mine** switch in Settings → Linked wallets and usernames (and a ✎ to give it a name); the owner's GMGN wallets are marked mine, anyone else's they look at are not. (2) The box at the top takes **All my wallets** (also offered in its list and as a link above the linked wallets; remembered). On it, the Home tiles show the **combined SOL and portfolio value** across the wallets marked mine, holdings merged by token, **GMGN's realized PnL summed** across them (7 and 30 days, the win rate weighted by tokens traded), and the balance history of the total; **Sync trades** syncs every wallet marked mine in turn, through GMGN. (3) **Stats, Recent trades and open positions** on All show every wallet's trades merged, each row carrying a wallet tag (the name given, else the FOMO handle, else the short address), and every number on the Stats tab runs over the merged set. (4) **Sizing** uses the combined balance on All and a single wallet's when one is picked. Editing a trade, notes, tags and deleting happen on the wallet that owns the trade; on All, Add trade asks which wallet. Picking one wallet in the box shows that wallet alone, as before. Nothing is copied between wallets: each keeps its own storage, and All is a view built from them (its own balance history lives under the pseudo wallet `all`). We know it works from unit tests (merging trades with the wallet tag and splitting them back without it, routing an untagged trade, summing balances with tokens merged by mint, summing GMGN stats with the weighted win rate, labels) and a browser test with faked RPC, prices and GMGN for two wallets (Mine switches, All my wallets, the combined tiles, the merged trade table with tags, Sync all, a name, a hand-added trade saved in its chosen wallet's own store, the choice surviving a reload, back to one wallet).

## Rules

- Logic in `wallets.js` (`mergeTrades`, `splitTrades`, `mergeOpen`, `sumBalances`, `sumStats`, `labelFor`, `isAllInput`, `ALL`); UI in `app.js` (`myWallets`, `allMode`, `walletKey`, `walletLabel`, `reloadMergedTrades`, `setAllMode`, `readSolWallet`, `gmgnStatsFor`, `gmgnSyncWallet`, `syncGmgn`, `renderProfiles`). Stored as `mineWallets` (addresses), `allWallets` (the view is on), `walletNames` (address → name).
- The per-wallet store gains `keyFor`, `getFor`, `setFor` (an explicit wallet). In the combined view `wstore.key` points at the pseudo wallet `all` (used for the balance history only); trades, open positions, hidden ids and the unit are read from each wallet marked mine and written back per wallet: `saveTrades` splits by the in-memory `wallet` tag (an untagged trade goes to the first wallet marked mine), a delete's hidden id goes to the trade's wallet, a sync writes each wallet's own store and the view reloads from them.
- The unit on All is USD when any wallet marked mine is USD (GMGN-synced wallets are). Trades entered in SOL on a SOL wallet would mix units; the sync note says so.
- `refreshBalance` reads every wallet marked mine in turn (`readSolWallet`) and sums them; GMGN stats are read per wallet and summed (`Wallets.sumStats`), remembered 10 minutes under the key `all`.
- Unticking the last Mine switch turns the combined view off. The Data card (backup, restore, clear) acts on the single wallet named in Settings → Advanced, not on the combined view.
- Not touched: Paper trading, the Calls tab, Check, FOMO mode (a FOMO username turns the combined view off).
