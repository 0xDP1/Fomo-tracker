# AI write-up styles

**Status:** approved 2026-10-18 (owner: "On ai write up can we make it similar to this style in 2nd pic and also another option to do for 3rd pic where it's more bullish thesis").

**Problem.** The AI write-up only ever wrote the risk-desk verdict. Sometimes you want the compact holder read that call accounts post (Nova's "$blocus … no snipers and insiders, cluster 4.2% on Bubblemaps, CEX cluster 38%, top 10 hold 19.5%, 1.48k holders, average bag $220. Nfa"), and sometimes the long bull case a FOMO thesis is (hihi33's $KNIGHT post: who is behind it, the catalyst, why it matters, "just another string to the bow"). The AI write-up card gets three chips, **Verdict · Holder read · Bull case** (remembered as `aiStyle`), and Ask Claude writes the one picked; each style's text is kept per coin (`checkState.aiBy[style]`) so switching back costs nothing, and a **Copy** button makes any of them paste-ready. **Verdict** is the original write-up, unchanged. **Holder read** gets the holder evidence the app already has and writes 3 to 5 short lines in Nova's shape: ticker and address, snipers and insiders from the bundle check and GMGN's labels (named as "per GMGN" / "per the bundle check"), the labelled top holders (smart money, KOLs, dev team, bundlers, insiders), the biggest linked cluster from the operator check, the share of the analysed top wallets first funded from an exchange (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Bybit; from the operator check's funders, a share of those wallets, not of supply), top 10 share, holder count and the average bag (market cap ÷ holders), ending "Nfa"; a block that was not run is null and left out, never guessed. **Bull case** gets the story evidence the thesis draft uses (name, ticker, links, the chat summary when read) and writes 2 to 3 short paragraphs in hihi33's shape: what the coin is and who is behind it, the catalyst or narrative and why it matters beyond crypto, what to watch next, with the risk check's verdict folded into one closing "Watch:" line so a Walk-away coin is never sold as safe; no stats, same no-invented-facts rule as the thesis.

## Rules

- Pure helpers in `writeup.js`: `STYLES`, `styleOf`, `system(style)`, `evidence(style, checkState, base, chat, story)` (the verdict gets `base`, the evidence `check-ui.js` already builds), `exchangeFunded(holderDeep)`, `maxTokens(style)`. The request stays in `askClaude()` (`check-ui.js`).
- The holder read's exchange-funded figure is a sample: the 15 wallets Analyze top wallets read, each wallet's first funder matched against the known exchange hot wallets. It is null until that button has run. Nova's figure comes from Bubblemaps, which has no free API.
- Average bag is market cap ÷ holders.
- Tests: `tests/writeup.test.js` (each style's evidence picks the right blocks and nulls unrun ones, the exchange-funded share, the three prompts' key lines) and `tests/e2e/writeup.e2e.js` (the chips, the verdict unchanged, the holder read's prompt and evidence with nothing guessed, Copy, the bull case's story and Watch rule, the text kept per style, the choice surviving a reload).
