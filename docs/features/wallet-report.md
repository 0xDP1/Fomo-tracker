# Wallet report

**Status:** approved 2026-10-12.

**Problem.** You find a wallet on GMGN and want to know whether it's worth copying, not just whether its owner makes money. On the Calls tab, **Analyze a wallet** takes a Solana address (or a GMGN wallet link). The app reads its recent buys and sells from GMGN, the same wallet-activity request Copy a wallet uses, at most 3 requests through the paced queue. It pairs each buy with its sells to show **trades per day, tokens traded, average size, hold time (median and share under 5 minutes), win rate, average return per token, market cap at entry, and fees as a share of profit**. Claude Sonnet (your Anthropic key, a few cents per tap) then reads those numbers and up to 40 sample trades and writes a **trading style** (sniper, scalper, swing, bot-like) and a **copy verdict: Copyable / Risky / Don't copy**, with reasons. For example: profits come from holds under a minute that you can't match with a delay; or profit per trade is smaller than your fees. A **Paper-copy this wallet** button adds it to Copy a wallet, so after a few days the Paper trades table shows what copying it actually earned. We know it works when unit tests on saved GMGN activity give the right trade pairs and stats, a test checks the prompt and reads the verdict, and a browser test shows the report and the paper-copy button.

## Rules

- Requests: two GMGN `user/wallet_activity` reads (`type=buy` and `type=sell`, 100 each). They go through the paced GMGN queue as foreground requests. The Worker's allowlist has no paging, so a busy wallet's report covers only its latest trades; the report says how many hours that is.
- Pairing (`WalletReport.pair` in `walletreport.js`): activity is grouped by token. Each token's buy price and sell price are USD-weighted averages of its buys and sells (GMGN `price_usd`, weighted by `cost_usd`). A token with at least one sell is **closed**: its return is sell price ÷ buy price − 1, and its hold time is first sell − first buy. Tokens with buys only are **open** and count towards size and trades, not win rate. Transfers and other event types are ignored.
- Market cap at entry: DexScreener's current market cap and price give the token's supply; entry market cap = buy price × supply. Up to 30 tokens (one free request). Tokens DexScreener doesn't know are left out.
- Fees: the copier's cost, not the wallet's. Each closed round trip costs the Fee drag card's round-trip cost (default 4%, the same setting) of its size. "Fees as a share of profit" = those costs ÷ the realized profit before costs; over 100% means copying loses money even at the wallet's own prices.
- Claude: model `claude-sonnet-5-5`, structured answer `{ style, verdict: copyable | risky | dont_copy, reasons[1-4], summary }`. The prompt gives the stats and up to 40 sample closed trades (return, hold minutes, size, entry market cap). Wallet data is treated as data, never instructions. Without an Anthropic key the stats still show and the verdict says it needs a key.
- Paper-copy adds the wallet to Paper trades → Copy a wallet (chain Solana), named after its short address, unless it is already followed.
- Field names (`event_type`, `price_usd`, `cost_usd`, `timestamp`, `token.address`) are the ones Copy a wallet already reads; that `cost_usd` on a sell is the USD received is from GMGN's docs and is checked on the first live call.
- Since v97 the report also runs the four copy gates from GMGN's wallet stats and all-time profits, shown above Claude's verdict and given to Claude in the prompt (`docs/features/wallet-gates.md`).
