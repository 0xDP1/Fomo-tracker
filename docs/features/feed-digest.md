# Feed digest

**Status:** approved 2026-10-16.

**Problem.** The on chain feed merges 3 to 8 group chats, so after a few hours away there is no way to know what the chats were on about, which coins several of them called, or what the mood was, short of scrolling hundreds of mixed messages ("What people are saying" covers one coin at a time). A **Feed digest** section on the Calls tab gets window chips **5 h · 10 h · 15 h · 30 h** and a **Summarize** button. On tap the Worker's new `/digest` route reads the on chain feed channel page by page back to the start of the window (newest 3,000 messages at most, trimmed like Chatter, nothing stored, reused for 15 minutes per window). The app sorts every message by the group code at the start of the poster's name, counts bot cards as scans, pulls every contract address and $ticker, and builds the structured part itself, no AI needed: **coins by how many chats mentioned them**, then mentions, with first and last mention times, the chats as coloured tags, and Check and Add to Call queue buttons; and **per chat**: messages, active posters, its top coins. Claude Haiku (the owner's Anthropic key, about 1 to 10¢ depending on the window) then writes the read: the mood, the narratives or metas the chats were on, one line per chat, the coins the chats agree on and why, and the red flags people mentioned, with the chat fenced as data it must never follow. No AI call without a key (the tables still show). The last digest is kept on the phone with its time, so reopening the app shows "digest of the last 10 h, run 25 min ago" until it is run again. We know it works from unit tests (grouping and coin extraction, the ranking by chats then mentions, scans counted not sent, a $ticker bound to the address written beside it, the prompt and reading the answer, the Worker's window check), a Worker test with a faked Discord channel (paging back to the window start, the short last page, the cache, the key and channel checks) and a browser test with a faked Worker and Claude answer showing the chips, the tables, the read, the buttons, the fold line, the digest kept across a reload and the no-key case.

## Rules

- Logic in `digest.js` (`build`, `label`, `prompt`, `readDigest`, `costCents`, `WINDOWS`), which uses `Chatter.groupOf` and `Calls.addressesIn`. UI in `digest-ui.js` (`runDigest`, `renderDigest`); state saved as `digest` without the chat lines. The Worker route `digest()` in `worker/index.mjs`, `Feed.digestRequest` in `worker/feed-core.js`; bundled by `node worker/bundle.js`.
- Window: whole hours, 1 to 48 (the chips offer 5, 10, 15, 30). The Worker reads `GET /channels/<CHATTER_CHANNEL>/messages?limit=100` and pages with `before=` until a message is older than the window, a short page, the 3,000-message cap or the cap in pages; `reachedAt` is the oldest message read and `capped` says the cap stopped it (the section then says how far back it reached).
- Coins: a message with one $ticker beside one address binds them; a $ticker alone is its own coin until some message binds it to an address, when it folds in. Bot cards credit their title's ticker with a scan, never a mention. Ranking: distinct chats, then mentions, then latest mention; the top 15 show (the count of all is given).
- Claude: `claude-haiku-5-5`, effort low, structured answer `{ mood, summary, narratives[≤6], groups[{code, take}], coins[{name, why}≤6], redFlags[≤8] }`; up to the newest 1,500 lines of 200 characters go in the prompt. The chat lines are fenced as data.
- Add puts the coin in the Call queue as a call by "digest" in channel "on chain feed" at its first mention time; Check opens the Check tab on it.
