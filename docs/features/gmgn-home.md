# GMGN home

**Status:** approved 2026-10-16 (owner: "I'm trading on GMGN now, not FOMO"; Solana only; a different wallet from the one in Settings).

**Problem.** The app still asked for a FOMO username, read trades and PnL through FOMO's API or Helius, and linked to FOMO, while the owner now trades on GMGN. A GMGN wallet is a plain Solana wallet, so the Home wallet view, Stats, Sizing, Check, Calls and Paper already key off the address; what was FOMO-shaped gets a GMGN shape. (1) The box at the top takes a **wallet address or a GMGN wallet link** (`gmgn.ai/sol/address/<wallet>`); the wallet is remembered under its short address in Linked wallets and usernames, with no FOMO handle. (2) **Sync trades from GMGN**: when the GMGN connection is available (the Discord feed Worker with its GMGN key, or the device's own key), Sync trades reads the wallet's buys and sells from GMGN's wallet activity (up to Sync pages of 100 each way, paged with GMGN's cursor through the Worker), pairs them per token into closed trades in USD at GMGN's own prices and open positions, keeps trades from earlier syncs, and sets the wallet's unit to USD; Helius stays the fallback without GMGN. The first sync runs on its own when a wallet with no trades is opened. (3) The Home's third tile shows **GMGN's realized PnL over 7 days**, with 30 days, the win rate, tokens traded and the SOL price under it (read at most every 10 minutes); without GMGN it stays the SOL price. (4) **Open in GMGN** on the Check tab and GMGN first on every coin row; FOMO buttons only when a FOMO key is set; thesis drafts no longer assume FOMO. FOMO code stays for anyone with a fomoapi key; nothing is deleted. Find a trader and Following still speak FOMO (next PR). We know it works from unit tests (the address or link parser; buys and sells paired into trades with partial exits, full exits, a sell before any buy, a new trip after an exit, notes kept by id, no amounts; the stats tile numbers), a Worker test (the paging cursor passes, odd ones are refused) and a browser test with faked RPC, prices and GMGN (the box with a link, the linked list, the first sync and its status, the trades and open position in USD, the tile, a second sync adding nothing, Open in GMGN and no FOMO button).

## Rules

- Logic in `gmgnsync.js` (`parseWalletInput`, `trades`, `stats`); UI in `app.js` (`openUser`, `syncGmgn`, `fetchGmgnActivity`, `refreshGmgnStats`, `renderWallet`), `check-ui.js` (`coinLinks`, `fomoOn`).
- Trips: a run of buys and sells of one token. It closes when 5% or less of the bought amount is left (or, when GMGN gives no amounts, when the sells are followed by a buy or end the list); a buy after a close starts a new trip. Sold some and still holding more than a tenth: an open position carrying that share of its cost (and `realized` so far). A sell before any buy in the window is skipped. Ids are `g_<last sell tx>`, so notes and tags survive re-syncs; `FomoBudget.mergeClosed` keeps earlier trades.
- Trades: `{ id, token, address, mint, chain: 'Solana', openedAt, closedAt, cost, proceeds, fills, source: 'gmgn', notes, tags }`, USD. Open positions `{ source: 'gmgn', token, address, mint, chain, cost, qty, openedAt, realized }`. `positionValueUsd` treats `gmgn` as USD.
- The Worker's `user/wallet_activity` allowlist takes `cursor` (`[A-Za-z0-9_.:=-]{1,300}`); the app passes GMGN's `next` back as `cursor` and stops at a short page.
- Stats tile: `GmgnSync.stats` reads `realized_profit`, `bought_cost` / `total_cost`, `unrealized_profit`, `buy`, `sell`, `pnl_stat.token_num`, `pnl_stat.winrate` (the field names GMGN documents for its CLI; not yet confirmed on the owner's wallet). A failed read is retried on the next refresh.
- Not confirmed live: GMGN's activity field names for the owner's own wallet (`event_type`, `token.address`, `cost_usd`, `token_amount`, `timestamp`, `tx_hash`, `next`), the same ones the Wallet report and Paper trades already read.
