# Graduations fee floor

**Status:** approved 2026-10-15.

**Problem.** A bundled coin can pass every Graduations filter because its market cap, volume and trades are all the bundle's own, and nothing in the check looks at what traders paid to get in. A bundle rug is bought by the deployer's own wallets in one block with almost no priority fees, so a coin that reached $10k with under 1 SOL of total fees paid had no real crowd fighting to get in; real demand shows up as fees. The Graduations filters get a **Fees paid at least N SOL** field (default 1 SOL, after Insentos' pump.fun Pulse filters). For each graduate that passes the market check, the app reads GMGN's total fees for the coin (one paced background request, the same `token/info` read the Strength score uses), converts them to SOL at the current price, and cuts coins under the floor with the reason "fees 0.3 SOL, under 1" (stage **Fees**, between Market and Holders). When GMGN is not set up, does not answer, gives no fee figure or the SOL price is unknown, the coin is not cut and the row says "fees not read", so missing data is never a pass in disguise but also never blocks the list for people without GMGN. The fee figure shows on each row past the market check ("fees 2.5 SOL"). 0 turns the floor off. We know it works from unit tests on the filter (under the floor cut, at or over kept, unknown kept, 0 off, the SOL conversion, the default and clamping) and browser tests with a faked GMGN answer and SOL price showing a cut coin with its reason, a kept one with its fees, one GMGN read per coin past the market check and none for a coin cut on the market, and the "fees not read" row without GMGN.

Source: Insentos' filters shown by @nickisback_ on X (2026-10-15): New pairs limited to Pump, Stonkfun and Bags with volume $50+; Final stretch market cap $15k+; Migrated market cap $10k+ and global fees paid 1 SOL+, "this filters out like all the bundle rugs". Only the Migrated rule applies here: the app does not list bonding-curve coins (a parked pump.fun idea in ROADMAP.md), and its other graduation filters are already stricter than his.

## Rules

- Logic in `grad.js`: `DEFAULTS.feesSol` (1), range 0–10,000 SOL with fractions kept, `feesInSol(feesUsd, solUsd)` (two decimals; null without either), `feeCut(feesSol, filters)` (null when off, unknown, or at or over the floor). UI in `grad-ui.js`: `gradFeesSol(mint)` reads `token/info` through `gmgnGet` as a background request (turned away while a Check waits or GMGN is paused: then "not read"), `Strength.gmgnTotalFees` finds the fee figure, the SOL price comes from the wallet reading or one Jupiter price request remembered for the session.
- The read happens only after the market check passes and only when the floor is above 0 and GMGN is available (Worker key or the device's key), so a coin cut on the market costs no GMGN request.
- The result row keeps `feesSol` (null when not read). The header line names the floor when it is on.
- GMGN's total-fee field name is not in their docs (`Strength.gmgnTotalFees` looks for `total_fee`, `total_fees` or `total_fees_usd` at the top level, under `stat` or `pool`); it has not been confirmed on a live graduate from the build machine. If GMGN sends no such figure the rows say "fees not read" and nothing is cut.
