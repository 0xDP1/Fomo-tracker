# Call lessons: what winners had in common

**Status:** approved 2026-10-12. The owner's ask: let AI look at the coins that come into the Call queue, see which ones gained big and why, and judge future calls by that. Memecoins play out in hours, so the timeline is hours.

**Problem.** Some called coins run and most die, but nothing recorded what the winners looked like *at the call* against the losers, so there was no way to tell which facts predict a runner; and peaks while the app was closed were missed. **What changes:** (1) every call that reaches the queue gets a **snapshot** of what the app already knows within 30 minutes of the call: minutes since launch, market cap, liquidity and its share, 1 h volume, 24 h turnover, holders and top 5 % (alert bot), bundled % (isitbundled), first caller's win rate, number of callers, S score, and for coins the queue checks automatically the rug score, top wallet, top 10, bundle still held and the top wallets' SOL (near-empty supply, wallets with 5+ SOL; one cheap balance read per checked coin). (2) **True outcome:** 1 h and 4 h after each call the app reads GeckoTerminal's free 5-minute candles for the coin's pool, so peaks and closes within 15 min, 1 h and 4 h count even when the app was closed. **Runner** = 2×+ within 4 h (**fast** within 1 h), **dead** = under 0.3× at 4 h or no pool, else **flat**. The same candles lift the Recent calls peak. (3) **Call lessons** (Calls tab): for each fact, the runners' median against the rest's and how often a runner's value beats another call's ("separates", 0–100%), best separating first; facts need 3+ runners and 3+ others. (4) **Read the patterns:** Claude Haiku (owner's Anthropic key, about 1¢) explains the table and the 5 best and worst calls from those numbers only. (5) With 30+ runners, a new call gets a **"n/5 like runners"** pill: how many of the 5 best separating facts it shares with past runners, set within 30 minutes of the call from calls whose outcome is already known; it gets a **Like past winners** row in the Signal scorecard and does not change the call score or Best view. The last 300 calls are kept, on the phone only. We know it works from unit tests (labels at each edge with faked candles: 2× at 59 min is fast, 0.29× at 4 h is dead; snapshots fill unknown facts for 30 minutes only; the ranking puts a predictive fact first and a useless one near 0; the pill is hidden under 30 runners) and a browser test (faked candles give outcomes for calls made while the app was closed, the table and Claude's read show, Recent calls takes the candle peak, a new call gets the pill).

## Unsure

- GeckoTerminal's free limit is about 30 requests a minute; the app reads at most 8 a minute, so a burst of calls is spread over a few minutes.
- "Separates" is a deliberately simple, readable measure (how often a runner's value beats another call's), not a trained model; with few runners it can show luck, which the card says.
- The candle price is GeckoTerminal's for the pool DexScreener ranks deepest; a coin that moves to another pool later is followed on the first one.
