# Call score and Best view (Call queue)

**Status:** approved 2026-10-10.

**Problem.** The Discord channels post far more coins than the owner can read, and the existing filters (minimum liquidity, caller win rate, bundled %) only remove coins without saying which of the rest are worth a look. Every coin in the queue now gets a **call score from 0 to 100** (`Calls.callScore` in `calls.js`), built only from data the queue already has, so it costs no extra requests: the first caller's record (30-day win rate, 20% scores nothing and 70%+ scores 24, plus 6/4/2 for a gold/silver/bronze medal, up to 30), traction (2/3/4+ callers 10/15/20, plus 10 for being in a channel whose label contains "traction", up to 25), a clean launch (bundled under 15% 10, under 30% 5, unknown 5; top 5 wallets under 20% 8, under 30% 5, unknown 3; liquidity at least 10% of market cap 7, at least 5% 4; up to 25), not already pumped (within 1.5× of the first call's market cap 10, within 3× 5, unknown 5) and freshness (first called within 2 hours 10, within 6 hours 5). Red flags subtract: Walk away 50, High risk 30, bundled over 40% 25, top 5 over 45% 25, a serial-bundler dev 15, and a 🌱 first caller with no record 10. The queue opens on a **Best** view showing only coins scoring 60 or more (adjustable, remembered), highest first, each with its score and its top reasons and red flags, plus "N more score under 60, Show all"; **All scores** shows every coin in the old order with scores. The automatic rug check goes to the highest-scoring coins first. We know it works from unit tests (a strong call scores high with the right reasons, an unknown single call stays near 35, each red flag and the pumped and stale rules, caller scaling) and a browser test of the Best view, its order, reasons, the hidden count, All scores, a stricter minimum and that both are remembered; then the owner uses it for a day and says whether the top 5 match what they would have picked.

## Unsure

- The point values are first guesses. The Signal scorecard records each signal's results, so after a week or two they can be retuned from the owner's own data.
- The alert's group per caller is not kept on queue entries, so "posted in more than one group" from the spec is not scored yet; the "traction" channel and caller count cover most of it.
- `worker/discord-feed.js` was rebuilt because it bundles `calls.js`; the Worker's own behaviour is unchanged, so it was not redeployed.
