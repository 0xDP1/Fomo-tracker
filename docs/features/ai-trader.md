# AI paper trader

**Status:** approved 2026-10-10 (model: Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude's pick at the owner's "ur pick").

**Problem.** The first AI picks only saw the call score; the owner wants the AI to paper trade using everything the app knows. With the switch on, each coin on the Best list (score at or above a minimum, 60 by default) gets the full check behind the scenes: the Check tab's own DexScreener, RugCheck/GoPlus and TrenchBot reads with the rug verdict and every finding, holder figures, GMGN holder labels, early traders and wallet memory (shared, cached requests), the bundle check, the caller's record, call score, channels and move since the call, and optionally the on chain feed chat (Flow stays off to save Helius credits). Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, structured output, server-side fallback on, effort medium) decides once per coin whether to enter and, if so, the size ($50, $100 or $200 of fake money), a take-profit multiple (1.3× to 20×) and a stop (10% to 50%) with a two-sentence thesis. Every 10 minutes it checks in on each open position with fresh market data, GMGN labels and wallet memory and decides hold, sell part or exit with a reason. Rails it cannot override: a hard stop at −50% and everything closed after 24 hours. A daily cap on AI calls (100 by default, adjustable) keeps the bill bounded. Every AI entry is also tracked under the fixed rules, so the results table shows **AI trader** against **Same entries, fixed exits**; each AI position shows its thesis, decision log and the evidence it saw. Nothing real is bought. We know it works from unit tests (reading and clamping plans and decisions, the AI position and its twin, AI target and stop, the hard stop and 24 hours, part sells and exits with the log, the daily cap, the evidence lines) and a browser test with every outside service faked: the strong coin entered with its plan and the weak one skipped, the full evidence in the prompt, a check-in that sells half, exits on price moves, the comparison rows, and the cap.
