    Position sizing

    Risk rules

    The dashboard warns you as you get close and tells you to stop once a rule is broken. Sizing drops too.

    Inputs

    Suggested size

    How it works. Risk-based: size = balance × risk% ÷ stop%, so if your stop hits you lose exactly your risk%. Kelly: f = W − (1−W) ÷ R, where W is your win rate and R is avg win % ÷ avg loss % from your own trades. Full Kelly is very volatile; most traders use ¼–½. If Kelly ≤ 0, your recent trades show no edge, so size down. This is math on your history, not financial advice.
